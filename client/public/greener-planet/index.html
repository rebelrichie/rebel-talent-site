Our mission

We recruit with AI. This is how we give some back.

Rebel Talent runs an AI-heavy recruiting operation. Every search, every agent, every model call runs in a datacenter somewhere, and that compute draws real power and water. We are not going to pretend it doesn't.

Sprig is our answer. We point the same technology at greener everyday choices, composting, food waste, energy, getting outside, and we give it away for free. It will not cancel out a datacenter, and we won't claim it does.

It is a standard we are holding ourselves to. If our work spends energy, our work should also help people spend less.

Recruiting runs on AI. Sprig is a small, honest way we point some of that same power at greener everyday choices.

Why Rebel built this

Sometimes the best use of AI isn't helping us work more. Sometimes it's helping us live better.

As AI keeps growing, and the datacenters behind it keep growing, we hope we all spend as much time asking how it can improve our communities, our health, and our environment as we do asking how it can lift productivity by 12%.
